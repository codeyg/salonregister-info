Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 18 November 2026

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information held by SalonRegister:

  1. Contact SalonRegister using the support email below with the subject Data Request.
  2. State your request, your salon name, and your role (owner or staff member). Provide only the minimum information needed to identify your account.
  3. We may verify your identity or authority before reviewing the request. We will explain the outcome and any information we must retain for legitimate legal or business reasons.

Do not send passwords, identity documents, or sensitive customer information with your initial request.

Data held independently by WhatsApp, other service providers, or salon owners in exported records may require separate requests.
